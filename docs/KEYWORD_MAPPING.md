> **Search demand has not been validated.** No keyword tool or Search Console data was available when this was written, so **no search volumes or difficulty scores are claimed**. Validate with Google Keyword Planner, Search Console (after launch) or a third-party tool, then re-prioritise.

## Cannibalisation notes

- "Textile sourcing company Pakistan" appears on both `/` and `/services/textile-sourcing`. The homepage targets the brand + head term; the service page targets the service intent. Watch Search Console, and if both rank for the same query, de-optimise one title.
- The product development service and article are deliberately different intents (commercial vs informational) and cross-link.
